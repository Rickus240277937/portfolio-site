About Rick Studio

I started Rick Studio while studying Multimedia Design at Cape Peninsula University of Technology. I kept seeing local businesses with websites that didn’t work properly on phones, so I began designing and building sites that are simpler and faster.

Most of my work is for small Cape Town businesses and startups. I like projects where I can see the constraint clearly and then design something that actually helps.
